# Capture from one Windows video stream

For the first field test, FotoHAVN treats every Windows `DeviceClass.VideoCapture` device as the same kind of Camera and takes each Capture from the first fresh frame received after the countdown through one long-lived, exclusive video stream. The coordinator prefers 1920 × 1080 at 30 fps and uses a bounded fallback down to 1280 × 720 at 15 fps; it does not use the Photo stream, exhaustive mode probes, Camera Tuning, persisted formats, proprietary Camera integrations, or per-Guest-Cycle reopening. This deliberately trades maximum still-image resolution and vendor-specific control for fast discovery, fewer device transitions, and a smaller recovery surface; a future high-resolution or vendor integration must be introduced as a separate adapter and decision.
