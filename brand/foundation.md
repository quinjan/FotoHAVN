# Foundation

## Who FOTOHAVN is

FOTOHAVN is an enclosed, vintage-inspired photobooth in the Philippines. It is a considered photography experience, not a commodity booth rental. Guests can drop by the booth for a pay-per-use visit, or hosts can rent FOTOHAVN for an event.

**Core idea:** photographs, developed differently.

**Brand promise:** a little room to be yourself, and a photograph to keep.

**Brand line:** Photographs, developed differently.

**Tagline:** A little room. A lasting feeling.

## Positioning

Editorial photography studio × vintage photo booth × modern boutique brand.

FOTOHAVN is:

- **nostalgic** but not costume-like;
- **premium** but not ornate;
- **intimate** but not dark or cinematic;
- **warm and a little playful** but never loud.

## Mood

Nostalgic · refined · intimate · warm · tactile · understated · gently playful · timeless

## FOTOHAVN must never feel like

- a generic party supplier;
- a discount rental catalogue;
- a wedding-template website;
- a nightclub or cinematic luxury brand;
- a gimmicky social-media app.

## The experience in three moments

The website's experience section tells the story in three moments. Every medium can use the same structure.

| Moment | Line | Support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Room** | Room to be yourself. | A private space, away from the crowd. |
| **Look** | A look that feels like you. | Choose the frame and photographic finish. |
| **Keepsake** | A memory made to last. | A FOTOHAVN moment, printed to last. |

The domain glossary's FOTOHAVN Brand Strip (see [`website/CONTEXT.md`](../website/CONTEXT.md)) names its panels Enclosed, Printed, and Distinctive. That object is not currently on the live site.

## Material world

FOTOHAVN's visual world comes from the real booth and its prints: a Jacobean Walnut enclosure, a soft cream curtain, a lit "PHOTOBOOTH" sign, a metal photo board with silver clips, and printed strips in people's hands. Color, type, texture, and imagery all point back to these objects. See [references/mood/](references/mood/README.md).

## Principles

1. **Photography first.** One real photograph leads each major moment. Interface, type, and decoration support it.
2. **A little, not a lot.** Restraint and generous space create the premium feel. The word "little" is part of the brand voice for the same reason.
3. **Tactile, not themed.** Use real wood, curtain, paper, and metal, plus a few real keepsake objects such as a bow, a ticket, or a charm. Never use clip-art props or party graphics.
4. **Editorial and warm.** Serif headings with an italic turn carry emotion. There are no badges, ribbons, or urgency.
5. **Invite, don't sell.** Offers are framed as invitations: "Ask about your date", "Find the booth".
6. **Considered for everyone.** Legibility and calm motion are part of the brand. See [accessibility.md](accessibility.md).

## Lineage

The website began from an editorial reference: generous breathing room, poster-scale hierarchy, flat surfaces, minimal elevation, and disciplined spacing. FOTOHAVN replaced that reference's pastel palette, geometric-only type, gradients, and illustration with earth tones, serif-led editorial type, tactile photography, and antique-metal details. The live website is the current expression of the brand. Where an older design document disagrees with it, the live website wins (see [README.md](README.md#authority)).

**Reject entirely:** confetti, loud multi-hue accents, generic pricing cards, excessive decorative icons, and party-supplier styling.
