# Logo

FOTOHAVN has two brand marks:

| Mark | Form | Master | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Wordmark** | `FOTOHAVN` in Cormorant Garamond 500, uppercase, tracking −0.05em | [`assets/logo/fotohavn-wordmark-ebony.svg`](assets/logo/fotohavn-wordmark-ebony.svg), [`assets/logo/fotohavn-wordmark-off-white.svg`](assets/logo/fotohavn-wordmark-off-white.svg) | Primary brand signature in every medium |
| **Booth-portal mark** | An open curtained booth doorway | [`assets/mark/fotohavn-booth-portal-master.png`](assets/mark/fotohavn-booth-portal-master.png) | Small square contexts: favicon, app icon, social avatar, watermark corner |

The wordmark SVGs are outlined from the typeset wordmark, so they render identically without the font installed. The booth-portal master is a 1024px raster. See [Open items](#open-items).

## Clear space

- **Wordmark:** keep clear space on every side equal to the height of the `O` (cap height). Nothing may enter this space: no text, no rule, and no photographic detail that competes with the mark.
- **Booth-portal mark:** keep clear space equal to one quarter of the mark's width.

## Minimum size

| Mark | Screen | Print |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wordmark | 80px wide (cap height of about 10px) | 20mm wide |
| Booth-portal mark | 16px. Use the reduced-palette favicon exports at 16–48px. | 6mm |

## Color

| Background | Wordmark | Booth-portal mark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Off-white, Warm Ivory, Cream Paper | Ebony | Master as supplied |
| Dark Walnut, Ebony | Off-white | Master on an Off-white tile |
| Photography | Off-white or Ebony, whichever passes 3:1 against the area behind the mark. Use the minimum directional overlay if neither passes. | Avoid; place on a solid tile |

Never set the wordmark in Muted Brass at small sizes, and never apply metallic or gradient fills.

## Lockups and applications

- **Wordmark alone** is the default. Website navigation sets it at about 30px (28px on mobile) at the left.
- **Oversized wordmark.** The website footer sets the wordmark across the full content width in Off-white on Ebony, as a closing brand moment. Posters, end cards, and closing slides may use the same move. Use it once per piece.
- **Wordmark + brand line.** The brand line, `PHOTOGRAPHS, DEVELOPED DIFFERENTLY.`, is set in the Eyebrow role. It may sit above the wordmark as a separate element, as in the footer, or one cap height below it, no wider than the wordmark.
- The booth-portal mark and the wordmark are not combined into a horizontal lockup. Use one mark per composition, unless a small mark sits inside a separate surface such as an avatar beside a post.

## Misuse

Never:

- stretch, condense, skew, rotate, or outline the marks;
- retypeset the wordmark in another font, weight, or case, or change its tracking;
- add shadows, glows, bevels, foil effects, or gradients;
- place the wordmark over a busy part of a photograph;
- enclose the wordmark in a badge, ribbon, circle, or pill;
- write the name as anything other than FOTOHAVN where a user will see it, for example "Fotohavn", "FotoHAVN", "Foto Havn", or "FOTOHVN" (see [voice.md](voice.md#the-name)).
