# Iconography

FOTOHAVN communicates with photography and type first. Icons are small, light, and functional.

## Family and style

- **Family:** [Phosphor](https://phosphoricons.com/), as used by the website.
- **Weight:** Light by default. Use Fill only for a small selected or recording state.
- **Size:** 18–20px beside text, and up to 28–36px for primary camera controls.
- **Color:** the color of the adjacent text, Ebony on light or Off-white on dark. Never use brass-only icons.
- Use one family per interpretation. Never mix in another icon set.

## Where icons are used

| Use | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Direction in actions** | Arrow after an action label: ↓ or ↘ in-page, ↗ external, → next, ← back, ↑ top. Use a Phosphor arrow or the text glyph. |
| **Functional controls** | Close, fullscreen, rotate, zoom, reset, previous/next, drag handle |
| **Camera flow** | Capture, switch camera, stop, mirror toggle, download |
| **Platform links** | Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, set as text with ↗ on the website |

## Rules

- Every icon-only control has an accessible name and a hit area of at least 44 × 44.
- Icons support a label or a universally understood control. They never replace a feature description.
- Platform logos, when used, appear in their official single-color form at label size.

## Never

- Decorative icon rows, icon bullets, or icon-led feature cards.
- Emoji in brand surfaces.
- Party or celebration icons such as balloons, confetti, sparkles, or party poppers.
